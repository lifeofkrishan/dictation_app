One-time setup

This downloads the speech model to your phone (about 200 MB, so use Wi-Fi) and asks for the microphone.

After that it works with no internet. Your voice is turned into text on this phone and is never sent anywhere.

Hold to talk

Hold and speak, then let go. Or tap once to keep it on.
